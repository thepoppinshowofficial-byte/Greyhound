TRAVEL QUESTIONS

Before you board.

How do I find a Greyhound trip?

Enter your departure city, destination and travel date above, then select Find Tickets to continue to the booking destination.

What should I check before booking?

Review the current fare, schedule, baggage allowance, seat options, departure station and ticket terms for your specific trip.

Can I change my trip?

Eligibility, fees and conditions vary by ticket. Review the current booking terms for the exact ticket you purchase.

What baggage can I bring?

Baggage allowances can vary depending on the trip and ticket. Always check the latest baggage information before travelling.
